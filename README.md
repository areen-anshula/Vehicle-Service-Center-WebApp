<div align="center">
  <h1>Vehicle Service Center Management System</h1>
  <p>A centralized, role-based platform designed to streamline automotive service center operations, built with PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.</p>
</div>

<hr>

<h2>⚙️ Core Features</h2>

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Role-Based Access Control:</strong> Distinct account permissions for Admins/Owners, Receptionists, Mechanics, and Customers.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Service and Inventory Control:</strong> Admin functionality to create, update, and delete service types (including pricing and descriptions) and maintain accurate spare parts inventory.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Customer Booking Workflow:</strong> Self-service registration for customers to add their vehicles (registration number, brand, model, year) and request service appointments with preferred dates, times, and notes.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Appointment Management:</strong> Receptionist interface to review, approve, reschedule, or reject pending customer appointments based on time slot availability.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Job Card and Assignment Tracking:</strong> Receptionists create job cards from appointments and assign them to mechanics. The system tracks the vehicle through fixed lifecycle states: Pending, Approved, Assigned, In Progress, Completed, and Closed.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Walk-in Processing:</strong> Capability for receptionists to manually create customer accounts, register vehicles, and generate immediate appointments and job cards for customers without prior bookings.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Mechanic Service Logging:</strong> Mechanics view assigned job cards, update the status to in progress, and record the exact services performed alongside any spare parts used from the inventory.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Invoice Calculation and Closure:</strong> Receptionists generate invoices based on the base service charges plus the cost of recorded spare parts, record the customer payment, and close the job card.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Customer Visibility:</strong> Customers receive notifications upon status changes and can log in to track active vehicle progress, view service history, and access past invoices.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Executive Monitoring:</strong> Admin view to monitor all daily job cards filtered by status, track total earnings from issued invoices, and oversee overall service center operations.
  </li>
</ul>
