Vehicle Service Center Management System
Core Features
Role-Based Access Control: Distinct account permissions for Admins/Owners, Receptionists, Mechanics, and Customers.

Service and Inventory Control: Admin functionality to create, update, and delete service types (including pricing and descriptions) and maintain accurate spare parts inventory.

Customer Booking Workflow: Self-service registration for customers to add their vehicles (registration number, brand, model, year) and request service appointments with preferred dates, times, and notes.

Appointment Management: Receptionist interface to review, approve, reschedule, or reject pending customer appointments based on time slot availability.

Job Card and Assignment Tracking: Receptionists create job cards from appointments and assign them to mechanics. The system tracks the vehicle through fixed lifecycle states: Pending, Approved, Assigned, In Progress, Completed, and Closed.

Walk-in Processing: Capability for receptionists to manually create customer accounts, register vehicles, and generate immediate appointments and job cards for customers without prior bookings.

Mechanic Service Logging: Mechanics view assigned job cards, update the status to in progress, and record the exact services performed alongside any spare parts used from the inventory.

Invoice Calculation and Closure: Receptionists generate invoices based on the base service charges plus the cost of recorded spare parts, record the customer payment, and close the job card.

Customer Visibility: Customers receive notifications upon status changes and can log in to track active vehicle progress, view service history, and access past invoices.

Executive Monitoring: Admin view to monitor all daily job cards filtered by status, track total earnings from issued invoices, and oversee overall service center operations.
